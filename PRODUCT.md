# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

TikTok TechJam judges and reviewers watching a two-to-four-minute demonstration,
plus technical reviewers who need to verify the prototype and its evidence.

## Product Purpose

Explain, demonstrate and substantiate a robust authentic-versus-AI image
ranker. Success means a reviewer can understand the problem, see the actual
runnable contract, distinguish verified evidence from limitations, and find
the public source/model artifacts without being misled by a single high score.

## Brand Personality

Forensic, direct, self-critical. The experience should feel like a confident
technical briefing under adversarial review, not a celebratory AI product ad.

## Anti-references

No generic SaaS landing-page cards, decorative AI gradients, fake dashboards,
neon cyberpunk styling, anthropomorphic detector claims, or “99% accurate”
marketing detached from the stated gate and metric. Do not copy the workshop
slides verbatim; retain only their high-contrast cyan/pink/black visual lineage.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before assertion: every prominent number states its gate or limit.
2. Show the falsification: the rejected ensemble and uncalibrated threshold are
   part of the story, not footnotes.
3. One idea per recording beat: problem, method, evidence, failure, live run.
4. Make the real command and output legible at video resolution.
5. Separate local, public and hidden states in both words and visual hierarchy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation, semantic landmarks, large video-
legible type, redundant color/text encodings, and reduced-motion support. Never
rely on cyan versus pink alone to communicate real versus generated.
